## Ingredients

* Instant Noodle.
* Water

## Instructions
1. Boil Water
2. Open lid
3. Add flavor
4. Pour hot water
5. Wait

## Serving Suggestions

* Avoid hot water burns.
* Avoid too Salty or too Spicy.
